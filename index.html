FEATURE 11 · LEAD THE DEMO WITH THIS

Dynamic re-routing

A normal maps app answers once. If the hillside moves at 02:40 while a convoy is between Kalijhora and Rangpo, that answer is now wrong and nothing tells the driver.

NORTHWATCH watches risk change under vehicles already in motion, detects when the committed route stops being the safest one, and recomputes mid-journey.

Watch the convoy commit to the valley road. Raise a disruption and the route re-solves onto the upper bypass without the driver asking.
